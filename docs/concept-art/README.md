# Concept-art brief

The conversation that initiated this repository established two visual directions. The binary generated images are not embedded in this initial text-only scaffold; this document preserves the production requirements they expressed so the art team can recreate/curate source assets with clear provenance.

## Concept A — modern single-arena remake

A colorful, premium stylized 3D reinterpretation of classic food-fight arcade action. Elevated isometric camera, compact bordered arena, scattered food, raised platforms, cartoon chefs/fighters, energetic food splats, and a central ice-cream objective. The concept demonstrated the desired sense of toy-like polish and food-comedy effects but was intentionally too visually dense for competitive multiplayer.

### Keep

- warm saturated food palette;
- chunky characters;
- exaggerated projectiles and impacts;
- premium lighting/material feel;
- obvious central dessert objective.

### Change for production multiplayer

- reduce permanent floor clutter;
- pull camera farther back;
- simplify environment contrast;
- make player silhouette/team markers stronger;
- shorten VFX persistence.

## Concept B — eight-player browser match

A browser-framed 16:9 screenshot showing eight named/color-coded players in a food-court arena around a giant central sundae. It used a compact score/timer at top center, roster/status across the top, objective helper at lower left, and minimap at lower right. Tomatoes and other food crossed the arena with colored motion arcs.

### Production interpretation

- Browser chrome is marketing-context only, not in-game UI.
- Eight players visible simultaneously is the readability benchmark.
- Central score/timer hierarchy is retained.
- Nameplates should be smaller than concept art and disappear/condense when crowded.
- Minimap is optional; test whether the compact arena already gives sufficient spatial context.
- Objective panel should transition from tutorial help to minimal status after onboarding.
